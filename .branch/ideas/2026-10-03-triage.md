# Triage

Status: raw
Captured: 2026-10-03

A decision step between intake and planning. `/branch-triage` classifies a work
item — one-shot fix / needs a plan / needs an answer / drop — checks it for
duplicates against `.branch/ideas/`, `.branch/plans/`, `.branch/work/` and open
PRs, and records the verdict on the work card. With no argument it processes
every card still at `Status: captured`. Read-only on code: triage writes cards,
never source files. From ROADMAP.md Phase 1, item 2 — the research calls triage
"a prompt plus a state label" and names unconditional intake the most common gap.
